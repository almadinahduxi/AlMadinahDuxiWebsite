Our courses

Online Quran courses, step by step

Five live courses that grow with the learner. Begin with the Qaida, move on to Nazra and Hifz, then deepen your understanding of Islamic rules and Hadith.

Stage 1 · Beginner

Ibtadai Qaida

Beginner's Quran reading primer

Every learner starts here. You learn the Arabic letters, their correct sounds and vowel marks, and how to join letters into words, so the Holy Quran can be read with confidence. We teach four trusted primers.

Each learner begins with the primer that suits their level and goals.

Madni Qaida

The Madani primer

Lessons arranged in a gradual order that builds smooth, confident reading flow.

Baghdadi Qaida

The traditional primer

The classic method. Learn the letter names first, then spell and join them into syllables and words.

Norani Qaida

The structured primer

Moves from single letters to joined letters, words and short ayahs, with close attention to makharij (letter sounds) and basic Tajweed.

Ahsanul Qawaid

The colour-coded primer (also spelled Hasanul Qawaid)

Colours highlight vowels and Tajweed signs. It also includes the Kalimas and the basics of wudu and salah.

Nazra

Reading the Quran by sight

Read the Holy Quran fluently from the Mushaf with correct pronunciation.

  • Fluent reading from the Mushaf
  • Makharij and Tajweed applied while reading
  • Steady practice with teacher feedback

Stage 3 · Memorisation

Hifz with Tajweed

Memorisation with correct recitation

Memorise the Holy Quran while keeping every letter and rule of recitation correct.

  • New lesson (Sabaq), recent revision (Sabqi) and long-term revision (Manzil)
  • Tajweed corrected during every recitation
  • A revision plan that fits the learner's pace
04

Stage 4 · Understanding

Islamic Rules

Essential rules of daily worship

Learn the rules every Muslim needs to know and practise, explained simply with examples.

  • Purification (wudu) and prayer (salah)
  • Fasting and everyday etiquette (adab)
  • Clear, simple explanations
05

Stage 5 · Wisdom

Lessons of Hadith

Sayings and teachings of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

Study selected Hadith with the Arabic text, an English translation and practical lessons for daily life.

  • Short, well-known Hadith first
  • Meaning explained in simple English
  • Lessons to apply at home, school and work
